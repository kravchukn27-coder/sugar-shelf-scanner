04

Camera screen — full anatomy

The screen you asked me to focus on. It's the plainest screen in the app by design: one guide frame, one hint, one shutter — nothing competes with the frame.

Scan your meal ⌐¬ to see its sugar impact 📸
1

Top corners — glass icon pair only

Flash (left) and close (right). No status pill, no live/ready label, no third control. Two buttons, both circular, both the same rgba-black-blur glass.

42px circle · rgba(20,18,24,.55) · blur(6px) · 1px rgba(255,255,255,.12) border
2

One guide frame, not per-object boxes

A single rounded rectangle centered in the upper two-thirds of the frame. Everything outside it is dimmed by the vignette; nothing inside is labelled until after capture.

1.5px rgba(255,255,255,.75) stroke · 26px radius · heavy outer vignette via box-shadow-inset trick
3

Hint pill straddles the frame's lower edge

Instructional copy ("Scan your meal to see its sugar impact") sits in a dark rounded pill that overlaps the bottom of the guide rather than floating free in open space — it reads as part of the frame, not a toast.

rgba(18,16,20,.78) fill · blur(6px) · fully rounded · centered, ~80% width
4

Bottom row is shutter-first

Gallery is a small rounded-square glass button, off to one side. The shutter is the only large, unambiguous, centered element on the whole screen — pure white filled circle, no icon inside it.

shutter 66px circle, solid white · gallery 42px rounded-square, 13px radius
